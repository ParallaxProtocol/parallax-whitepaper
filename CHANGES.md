# Changes from the First Edition

The second edition (August 2026) is a rewrite of the whitepaper as published at launch. The network it describes is unchanged: no consensus rule, parameter, or monetary constant differs from what mainnet runs. The changes are to the document.

- **Title.** "Parallax: A New Perspective on Programmable Cash" became "Parallax: A Peer-to-Peer Programmable Cash System." The parallax metaphor moved into the body (Section 7), where it describes what the network actually does: establish truth by independent observation.
- **Voice and structure.** The paper is rewritten in the structure and register of the Bitcoin whitepaper. Censorship resistance, permissionlessness, and pseudonymity are now the organizing frame rather than a comparison of feature sets.
- **Tense.** The first edition was written before launch and described the network in the future tense. The second edition describes a running network, and its launch sections are replaced by the historical record: date, embedded headline, genesis hash.
- **Difficulty.** The first edition described the 2016-block windowed retarget the chain launched with. The second edition documents ASERT (aserti3-2d, two-day half-life), active since block 17,560, and records why the migration happened.
- **Governance.** The first edition described interim stewardship with a planned transition to the community. The second edition states the current fact: there is no governance, no foundation, no roadmap, and no scheduled forks.
- **New sections.** Design constraints distilled from the Parallax Doctrine; a network section deriving censorship resistance from topology; a privacy section; an appendix of protocol constants.
- **Removed sections.** Use cases and energy considerations.

The first edition remains at [editions/first-edition.md](editions/first-edition.md), byte-identical to what was published, and is tagged `first-edition` in this repository's history.

Editions are immutable once published. Errors in this edition will be corrected by a third edition, never by editing this one.
